Big Dallas–Fort Worth events we track every year

Alongside one-off concerts and games, these North Texas favorites hit the server every year. Dates shift a little year to year, so check the server for this year's exact dates.

  • Sep
    –Oct

    State Fair of Texas

    Fair Park, Dallas. Big Tex, the Texas Star, Fletcher's corny dogs and the fried-food contest.

  • Oct

    Red River Rivalry

    Texas vs. Oklahoma at the Cotton Bowl in Fair Park, one of college football's great traditions.

  • Jan
    –Feb

    Fort Worth Stock Show & Rodeo

    Dickies Arena and Will Rogers Memorial Center. Weeks of rodeo, livestock and Western heritage.

  • Sep
    –Nov

    Autumn at the Arboretum

    Dallas Arboretum's famous pumpkin village and fall gardens on White Rock Lake.

  • Oct

    ArtsGoggle

    Fort Worth's Near Southside street festival on Magnolia Avenue with local art, music and food.

  • May

    Main Street Fest

    Historic downtown Grapevine's spring festival with live music, a carnival and craft beer.

  • Sep

    GrapeFest

    Grapevine's wine festival, one of the largest in the Southwest.

  • All
    year

    Mavs, Stars, Cowboys, Rangers & more

    Home games at American Airlines Center, AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field, plus FC Dallas and the Wings.

Just want Dallas? Dallas Discord has the city-only rundown. Plus big tours at American Airlines Center, Dickies Arena, The Pavilion at Toyota Music Factory, Dos Equis Pavilion and the clubs of Deep Ellum.

Frequently asked questions

What is DFW Discord?

DFW Discord is a free Discord server for people in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. Our bot, Big Tex, posts big local events like concerts, Mavericks, Stars and Cowboys games, festivals and the State Fair of Texas, and members RSVP and plan to go together.

Is DFW Discord free to join?

Yes. Joining the server is free. All you need is a Discord account, which is also free.

Do I have to live in Dallas or Fort Worth?

No. Anyone in the metroplex is welcome, from Denton to Waxahachie and Weatherford to Rockwall, plus anyone visiting North Texas who wants to know what's going on.

How do I find people to go to an event with?

Every event post has Going and Interested buttons and its own thread. Hit Going and you're added to the thread, where people plan carpools, meetup spots and tickets.

What kinds of events get posted?

Big ones: major concerts, pro and college sports, festivals, the State Fair of Texas, the Fort Worth Stock Show & Rodeo, and other events worth leaving the house for across Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Frisco, Plano, Irving, Grapevine and the rest of DFW.

How do I see what's happening this weekend in DFW?

Every Thursday evening the server gets a This Weekend in DFW roundup of the top events Friday through Sunday. You can also type /weekend in the server any time.
