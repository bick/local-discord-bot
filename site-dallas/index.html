Dallas events the server lights up for

One-off concerts and games get posted as they're announced. These Dallas traditions come around every year. Exact dates change year to year, so check the server.

  • Sep
    –Oct

    State Fair of Texas

    Fair Park. Big Tex himself, the Texas Star Ferris wheel, Fletcher's corny dogs and the fried-food awards.

  • Oct

    Red River Rivalry

    Texas vs. Oklahoma at the Cotton Bowl, split down the 50-yard line in burnt orange and crimson.

  • Oct
    –Jun

    Mavericks & Stars home games

    American Airlines Center in Victory Park, plus Dallas Wings games all summer.

  • Sep
    –Nov

    Autumn at the Arboretum

    The Dallas Arboretum's pumpkin village on White Rock Lake.

  • Spring

    Dallas Blooms

    Hundreds of thousands of spring bulbs in bloom at the Dallas Arboretum.

  • Spring

    Deep Ellum Arts Festival

    Art, live music and food trucks filling the streets of Dallas's music district.

  • Mar

    St. Patrick's Day on Greenville Ave

    The parade and block party on Lower Greenville, one of Dallas's biggest street parties.

  • All
    year

    Concerts across Dallas

    American Airlines Center, The Bomb Factory, Dos Equis Pavilion, House of Blues, the Majestic Theatre and the Deep Ellum clubs.

Looking past Dallas? The server covers Fort Worth, Arlington, Frisco and the rest of the Metroplex too. See DFW Discord for the full DFW events lineup.

Dallas Discord FAQ

Is there a Discord server for Dallas?

Yes. Dallas Discord is a free Discord server for people in Dallas, TX. Our bot, Big Tex, posts big local events like Mavericks and Stars games, concerts in Deep Ellum and at American Airlines Center, and the State Fair of Texas, and members RSVP and plan to go together.

How do I meet people in Dallas through Discord?

Pick an event in the server, hit Going, and you're added to that event's thread. People there plan carpools, pregames and meetup spots, so you show up with a group instead of alone.

Is the Dallas Discord server free?

Yes. Joining is free and so is a Discord account.

What's there to do in Dallas this weekend?

Every Thursday evening the server gets a This Weekend roundup of the top events Friday through Sunday. You can also type /weekend in the server any time for an up-to-date list.

Does it cover Fort Worth and the suburbs too?

Yes. The same server covers the whole Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, including Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco and Irving. See dfwdiscord.com for the full DFW picture.
